Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

1. Who We Are

Divine Frequency HQ is a Christian faith-based content channel operated by Sebby IT Consulting, Corp. This Privacy Policy applies to our personal content automation tool used to publish videos to social media platforms.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

This App does not collect, store, or process any personal data from third-party users. It is a private, single-user automation tool.

3. TikTok Authorization

The App uses TikTok's OAuth 2.0 flow to obtain an access token for the purpose of posting video content to the Divine Frequency HQ TikTok account. This token is:

  • Stored locally and securely as an environment variable
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Used exclusively for publishing content via the TikTok Content Posting API

4. Data Retention

No personal data from any user is retained. OAuth tokens are refreshed automatically and stored only in encrypted environment secrets.

5. Third-Party Services

Content is published to TikTok via their official API. Please refer to TikTok's Privacy Policy for information about how TikTok handles data.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us via the Divine Frequency HQ YouTube channel.
